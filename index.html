Terms & Conditions

Nox is a sleep tracking and sleep planning application designed to help you understand your sleep debt and plan your rest around your real schedule.

All calculations, plans, and recommendations Nox provides are estimates intended purely to help you understand and plan your sleep. Nox is not a medical device, and nothing in the app is a medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice. If you have concerns about your health or sleep, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

The accuracy of Nox's results depends entirely on the information you provide — including your profile details and the sleep you log. Providing accurate, up-to-date information will produce more appropriate results. You're free to update or correct any of your information at any time from the Me tab.

By using Nox, you agree to use the app for personal, non-commercial sleep-planning purposes, and you understand that Nox does not guarantee any particular health outcome.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Nox collects, why, and the rights you have over it.

What we collect

Account details (username, password), personal information (name, birth date, gender, exercise frequency), sleep preferences (target wake-up time, usual bedtime), sleep records you log, event information, and an optional profile photo.

Why we use it

To create and manage your account, build your personalized sleep profile, calculate sleep debt and statistics, generate your tonight plan and recovery plan, and provide personalized AI advice. We only use your information for purposes described in this policy.

Where it's stored

In this version of Nox, your data is stored locally in your browser on this device. We take reasonable steps to help protect it, though no storage method can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Your rights

You may access, correct, or delete your information at any time — the Me tab lets you edit your profile, and "Clear all data" removes everything Nox has stored for you on this device. You can also ask questions at any time about how your information is used.
